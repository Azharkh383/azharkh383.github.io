Free MOT Check UK – Frequently Asked Questions

Learn about MOT status checks, MOT expiry dates, vehicle registration searches and MOT test history.

How do I check the MOT history of a vehicle?
Enter the vehicle registration number and select Check Vehicle when the lookup service is connected. Results depend on the data source available to the service. You can also use the official GOV.UK MOT history checker to view government records.
What is the MOT expiry date?
It is the date the current MOT certificate runs out, as recorded for the vehicle.
What are advisories?
Advisories are items a tester noted that did not cause a fail but may need attention in future. They appear only where the test record includes them.
Why is some information missing?
A report only contains what the data source returns. Missing items are labelled as unavailable rather than guessed.
What can I check with an MOT checker?
Depending on the records available, an MOT checker can help you review MOT status, expiry date, past test results, mileage readings, failures and advisories. Use GOV.UK to verify official MOT information.
Can an MOT history check help when buying a used car?
Yes. Reviewing past MOT test results, mileage history, failures and advisories can help you decide what questions to ask before buying. It is not a substitute for an independent mechanical inspection or a complete vehicle history check.
Does an MOT pass mean the vehicle is mechanically perfect?
No. An MOT result relates to testable items at the time of the test. It is not a guarantee of the vehicle’s current condition or a substitute for a professional inspection.
How far back does MOT history go?
For cars, motorcycles and vans in Great Britain, official online MOT history is generally available for tests since 2005. Northern Ireland and other vehicle classes can have different record coverage. Check GOV.UK for the current scope.
Can MOT history confirm whether a car has outstanding finance or has been written off?
Not by itself. MOT history is focused on MOT test records. Finance and insurance write-off checks require separate, appropriate data sources and should only be advertised if this service actually provides them.
Why should I compare mileage readings over time?
Comparing readings in date order can help you spot inconsistencies that deserve further investigation. A discrepancy can have different causes and does not, by itself, prove odometer tampering.
Is this an official government website?
No. UK Vehicle Check is an independent service. You can cross-check any result with the official GOV.UK MOT history checker .
What should I do if my car fails its MOT?
Read the exact defects on the test paperwork and arrange repairs. Whether you can drive the vehicle away depends on whether its existing MOT is still valid and whether dangerous defects were recorded. Check the official MOT test result guidance .
Can I drive a car with an expired MOT?
Generally, you cannot drive or park a vehicle on the road without a valid MOT when one is required. Exceptions include driving to a pre-arranged MOT test or to have repairs carried out, but the vehicle must still be roadworthy. Read the official MOT guidance .
When does a new car need its first MOT?
Most cars need their first MOT by the third anniversary of first registration, then annually. Some vehicle types have different rules, and Northern Ireland has separate processes. Check the official guidance for your vehicle.
Are any vehicles exempt from MOT testing?
Some vehicles can qualify for an exemption, but eligibility has conditions and may depend on modifications and vehicle class. Do not assume a vehicle is exempt based only on age; verify it using official historic vehicle guidance .
Does an MOT check tell me whether the car is taxed?
An MOT history check does not by itself confirm vehicle tax status. Use the separate GOV.UK vehicle tax checker . UK Vehicle Check does not currently claim to return live tax results.
Can I download an MOT certificate online?
The official GOV.UK MOT history service supports downloading current and previous MOT certificates for eligible vehicles in Great Britain. Northern Ireland has different arrangements. Use the official MOT history service to check availability.

How far back does MOT history go?

For cars, motorcycles and vans in Great Britain, records are generally available from 2005. HGVs, trailers, buses and coaches have records from 2018. Northern Ireland has a separate service with records from 2017.

Does an MOT pass guarantee a car is safe?

No. It records the result at a particular test and is not a guarantee of the vehicle’s current condition. Keep the vehicle maintained and consider an independent inspection.

HELP CENTRE

More answers about MOT checks

These general answers explain how to use a record responsibly. For an individual vehicle, verify details with the official service.

Does an MOT pass mean a car is in perfect condition?

No. It records the result of an inspection against MOT requirements on that date. It is not a guarantee of overall condition, future reliability or the absence of mechanical faults.

Why should I look at old advisories?

They can reveal wear or issues that may need monitoring. Repeated advisories deserve follow-up, especially if the seller cannot provide evidence of repairs.

What should I do if mileage looks wrong?

Check the sequence of MOT readings, compare them with the odometer and service invoices, and ask for an explanation. Do not assume a discrepancy is proof of fraud without checking the facts.

Can I use an MOT check before buying a used car?

Yes, it is a useful part of due diligence. Also inspect the vehicle, take a suitable test drive, review service records and consider an independent inspection.

Does this website retrieve live DVSA records?

Only if a valid authorised data source and secure backend have been configured. Without that connection, this site provides guidance rather than verified live results.
